Before you apply

Common questions

Do I need to pay anything beyond Patreon?

No additional catalog enrollment fee is planned for eligible paid beta members. Your qualifying $5 or $10 Patreon membership is required.

Do I give Sync Opps. any music rights?

No exclusive rights are requested for catalog participation. Members or their authorized representatives handle approvals and license agreements directly with buyers.

Will I be guaranteed a license or buyer inquiry?

No. The catalog creates a way to discover and contact approved members, not a promise of buyer traffic, an offer, or a placement.

What if my song becomes unavailable?

Members can request a pause or withdrawal. We remove unavailable songs from the catalog once the request is verified and processed, without restricting their licensing elsewhere.

Does applying guarantee one of the 40 places?

No. We verify paid membership, application identity, and availability before confirming an opening. The first 40 refers to accepted members, not total form submissions.

What music services can I submit?

Once accepted into the beta, you can submit songs using links from SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Spotify, YouTube and Apple Music, or upload an authorized MP3 listening preview (up to 10 MB) directly through our song submission form. No separate music hosting or embed code is needed for an MP3 upload. All submitted music and previews must be reviewed and approved before they can play publicly in the catalog.

Must I make my private master recording public?

No. If an existing private track cannot be embedded, you can supply a separately authorized listening preview on a supported service. Approved previews are accessible to catalog visitors, so do not upload material you cannot allow the public to hear.
