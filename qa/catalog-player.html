Important before publishing

  • A player loading is not proof that the audio works. Press Play and listen from a signed-out browser.
  • SoundCloud public does not mean embeddable. If it says “This track can’t be played outside of SoundCloud,” the recording is blocked from outside playback. Its owner can review SoundCloud track Advanced Settings → Permissions → Display embed code, but approval is not automatic. Ask for another authorized embeddable preview if the restriction remains.
  • Unlisted does not mean secret after publication. Anyone viewing the catalog may be able to inspect or share the embedded source link.
  • Some Spotify and Apple Music embeds only offer limited previews or require a listener account for full playback. The site must describe what buyers can actually hear.
  • Bandcamp requires its official player reference. A regular track page address is not itself an embedded player.
  • DISCO and Untitled share/library pages are not embed URLs. Use their official iframe code if the owner has enabled embedding.
  • Google Drive and Dropbox previews are experimental. The link must allow unsigned visitors to access the audio. These sources are not supported by the live catalog feed yet and should not be used to publish a song.
  • Spotify albums are not track-specific. For an individual song listing, supply the individual Spotify track link.
  • A direct MP3 must be hosted on a permitted HTTPS source. The provider must authorize hotlinking and public previews.
  • Rights, split-display consent, paid membership, licensing authority and buyer routing are reviewed separately from audio playback.
Privacy: The pasted link is processed in this browser tab and is not saved to GitHub or our private database. When you load a player, your browser requests it from the music provider. The normalized reference can be copied for review, but this action never changes a song's status.
